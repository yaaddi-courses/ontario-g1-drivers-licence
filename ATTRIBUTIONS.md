## Diagrams

The images in `source/images/diagrams/` (traffic lights, pedestrian signals and pavement markings) are original artwork drawn for this course with Python and Pillow (`make_diagrams.py` in the authoring folder). They show the standard layouts described in the Official MTO Driver's Handbook and carry no third-party copyright.

Deck covers and the course cover were generated locally with FLUX.1-schnell from original prompts; no third-party artwork.
